1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the JIRA AI Task Generator Chrome extension and related product pages. It covers information processed when you use the extension, visit its landing page, or contact the developer about the product.

2. Information the Product May Process

Content you provide

Prompts, feature notes, requirements, copied text, and any other content submitted so the tool can generate JIRA tickets, descriptions, acceptance criteria, or subtasks.

Generated output

Structured ticket content created in response to your input, such as titles, summaries, implementation details, and task breakdowns.

Technical information

Basic operational data like timestamps, error details, approximate usage volume, browser or extension version information, and diagnostic logs needed to keep the service working.

Support information

If you contact the developer directly, your name, email address, and the contents of your message may be processed to respond to your request.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To generate and format JIRA-ready task content requested by the user.
  • To improve reliability, fix bugs, investigate failures, and maintain product performance.
  • To detect abuse, protect the service, and preserve basic platform security.
  • To reply to support inquiries, feedback, or privacy requests.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

JIRA AI Task Generator may rely on third-party providers such as AI APIs, hosting infrastructure, analytics, or browser platform services in order to operate. Information sent to those providers is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Information is not sold as part of this product page. Data may be disclosed if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce terms, protect users, or maintain the security and integrity of the service.

5. Data Retention and Security

  • Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the product, support users, resolve technical issues, or meet legal obligations.
  • Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.
  • No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You can stop using the extension at any time by uninstalling it from your browser. If you have shared personal information directly through support or contact channels, you may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Requests can be sent using the contact information below. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

7. Children's Privacy

JIRA AI Task Generator is intended for general productivity and professional use. It is not directed to children under 13, and personal information should not knowingly be collected from children through this product.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect product changes, operational needs, or legal requirements. When updates are made, the effective date on this page will be revised.
