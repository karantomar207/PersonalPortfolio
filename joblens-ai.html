Core Features

Built to help job seekers evaluate opportunities quickly and apply with more confidence.

Resume to Job Matching

Compares saved resume content with live job descriptions and estimates role fit.

Skill Gap Detection

Highlights missing skills and helps prioritize what to learn or showcase next.

Fast Chrome Workflow

Designed as a lightweight extension with local resume storage and in-page results.

The Problem

  • Job seekers often apply without knowing how well their profile matches the role.
  • Important missing skills and ATS gaps are easy to overlook in long job descriptions.
  • Manual comparison between resume and job posting takes time and creates friction.

The Solution

JobLens AI gives users a focused pre-application decision layer right inside the browser.

  • Analyze the job posting instantly
  • Compare against the resume
  • Surface strengths and missing areas
  • Recommend how to improve the application

How It Works

  1. Step 1

    Save your resume

    Upload or paste resume content once for repeated use.

  2. Step 2

    Open a job page

    Navigate to a supported job listing and let the extension detect the role.

  3. Step 3

    Run analysis

    Job and resume data are processed to generate match insights and recommendations.

  4. Step 4

    Improve before applying

    Use the output to refine your resume and prepare more strategically.

Privacy Approach

  • Resume content can be stored locally in the browser for convenience.
  • When analysis is requested, resume text and selected job data may be sent to backend services.
  • The product is designed to be transparent about what is processed and why.
